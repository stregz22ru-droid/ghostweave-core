# GHOSTWEAVE MANIFESTO

## The Crisis of Trust
In an era where Artificial Intelligence generates decisions at scale, the origin of those decisions is becoming opaque. We face a fundamental crisis: **how can we trust a system whose reasoning process is invisible, unrecorded, and irreproducible?**

Current infrastructure treats AI outputs as "final answers." It discards the context, the data provenance, and the decision path. This creates a "Black Box" society where accountability is impossible.

## Our Vision
We envision a world where **Knowledge remains inspectable across time.**

GHOSTWEAVE is not just a library; it is an **Accountable Knowledge Infrastructure**. It provides the foundational layer for AI systems to record their existence, prove their origins, and allow for independent verification of their behavior.

## Core Beliefs

1.  **Replay before Trust:** If a decision cannot be reproduced from its original inputs, it should not be trusted.
2.  **Memory ≠ Authority:** Interpretations are advisory; only the immutable record of events is canonical truth.
3.  **Provenance as First-Class Object:** Every piece of knowledge must carry a cryptographic link to its source.
4.  **Conflict Preservation:** Disagreements and alternative interpretations are valuable data points that must be preserved, not erased by automated consensus.

## The Promise
GHOSTWEAVE Core will remain **domain-agnostic**. It does not know what "medicine" or "finance" is. It knows only **Events**, **Hashes**, and **Identities**. 

By keeping the Core pure, we ensure that trust can be built in any industry, by any actor, on a shared foundation of mathematical certainty.
